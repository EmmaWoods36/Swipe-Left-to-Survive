SWIPE LEFT TO SURVIVE — SELF-ACCEPTANCE GODDESS AMY HANDOFF
EN / JP TRANSFORMATION, MOVESET, SPRITES, AND ATTACK EFFECTS

CHARACTER NAME
EN: Self-Acceptance Goddess Amy
JP: 自己受容の女神 Amy

STORY FUNCTION
Self-Acceptance Goddess Amy is Amy’s final transformation during the Pattern battle.
This form should unlock only after the Pattern has pushed Amy hard enough that normal Amy cannot win.
The transformation is not “Amy stops wanting love.”
The transformation is “Amy stops abandoning herself for love.”

LOCKED TRANSFORMATION TEXT
EN:
Amy remembered who she was.
Not because she stopped wanting love.
Because she stopped abandoning herself for it.

JP:
Amyは、自分が誰なのかを思い出した。
愛を欲しがらなくなったからじゃない。
愛のために、自分を捨てるのをやめたからだ。

BATTLE DESIGN NOTES
- Goddess Amy is for the Pattern battle / endgame self-love climax.
- She should feel radiant, divine, soft, powerful, and emotionally clear.
- Palette: pink, lavender, gold, and soft white.
- She is still Amy, but elevated into self-acceptance, boundaries, clarity, and power.
- This is not a villain form. It is Amy choosing herself.
- In side-view battle, Amy faces right toward the Pattern.
- Use the generated sprites as story-mode/turn-based RPG battle poses.
- Use the generated attack effects as code-animated overlays/projectiles.
